Tone pitch and exact frequency

The center tone sets the audible pitch; the beat frequency is the difference between ears. A 220 Hz center and 10 Hz beat gives 215 Hz left and 225 Hz right. Pitch is a comfort control, not a “healing frequency.”

Not a promise to reprogram your brain.

There is no scientifically established “best” frequency for focus or relaxation. The presets are adjustable starting points. This prototype has not been clinically tested.

Focus · 40 Hz · experimental

A 2025 study of 80 participants found some improvements in average attention with 40 Hz beats and a 340 Hz center tone, but no convincing protection against attention declining over time. A separate 58-person study found no attention or anxiety benefit. Our music mix and listening levels do not reproduce either protocol. At 40 Hz, a clear binaural-beat sensation is not assured; perception varies.

Relaxed focus · 10 Hz · exploratory

Alpha-band labels are not a proven recipe for a mental state. A 2023 review of 14 EEG studies found inconsistent evidence for brainwave entrainment. We use 10 Hz as an adjustable, familiar starting point—not a validated optimum.

Relax · 6 Hz · exploratory

A 2022 trial with 163 participants taking anxiety medication found benefits for a combined music / theta-beat intervention in a moderate-anxiety subgroup. The study was open-label, effects varied by subgroup, and music alone also helped. It does not establish 6 Hz as best or validate this app.

Why offer beats at all?

A 2019 meta-analysis of 22 studies reported a pooled effect (Hedges’ g = 0.45) across cognition, anxiety, and pain outcomes. That supports further investigation—not one guaranteed frequency or a treatment claim.

For your TV: true binaural listening requires a separate tone in each ear. Our speaker mode uses smooth amplitude modulation, a different stimulus. Binaural findings cannot be transferred to it. “Just ambient” removes the beat layer entirely.

Use it comfortably.

Begin at a quiet device volume. Stop if the sound causes discomfort, dizziness, headache, or ringing. Do not use relaxation sessions while driving or doing work that needs full situational awareness. This is not a medical treatment or a substitute for care.

App volume is relative: actual listening exposure depends on your device, speakers or headphones, and listening time.

Install & use anywhere

On iPhone / iPad, open the hosted app in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. On supported desktop and Android browsers, use Install app.

Offline caching requires the full PWA folder served over HTTPS or localhost.

Browser limits & shortcuts

Tap Play once to enable audio. Mobile browsers may suspend synthesized audio when the screen locks or the app is backgrounded; dependable lock-screen playback is not promised. TV-browser support for full screen, installation, and wake lock varies.

Space: play / pause. F: TV mode. M: mute. Escape / TV Back: close controls, then leave TV mode. Arrow keys move focus; Left / Right adjusts a focused slider. Enter / OK activates a button.

The app does not pair a phone as a TV remote. Use the TV remote, an attached keyboard, or the TV browser’s pointer. No Spotify / YouTube account is needed.
